## 4. Key learnings (3:00–4:00)

[滚动到 Key learnings]

Across my degree, three things stand out.

One: a good team project needs three things at once — a reasonably detail plan, a team where people can talk openly, and everyone can clearly understand and delivering their own part. Lose any one, and the whole game will not be completed.

Two: agree on boundaries before splitting the work. If people aren't clear where their part starts and ends, work overlaps may happen and work  missing may happen as well. Which means and you usually find out at integration, when it's too late.

Three, on the technical side: materials are the same ideas in different tools. Take a dissolve effect. In Unreal, I compare a noise texture with a parameter and feed it into Opacity Mask. In Unity Shader Graph, the noise goes into Alpha and the parameter into Alpha Clip Threshold. In a hand-written shader, it's one line — clip(noise minus cutoff). Once I understand the idea, I can move it between engines.
